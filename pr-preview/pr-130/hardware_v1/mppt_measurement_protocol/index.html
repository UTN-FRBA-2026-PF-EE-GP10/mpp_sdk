    MPPT Measurement Protocol & Closed-Loop Telemetry Flow¶

    1. Problem Statement: Higher Voltage Drift (\(V > V_{\text{mpp}}\))¶

    During bench tests with MPPT algorithms (such as Perturb & Observe, Incremental Conductance, and Fuzzy Logic), algorithms were observed drifting towards higher terminal voltages than the Maximum Power Point (MPP), eventually climbing towards open-circuit voltage (\(V_{oc}\)).

    Root Cause Analysis¶

    1. Full-Duplex SPI Pipelining Delay:

    2. The SPI bus operates in full-duplex. When the Host/sbc clocks out a 12-byte MOSI packet containing a new duty cycle \(D_{k}\), the Pico simultaneously clocks out a 12-byte MISO packet that was loaded into the TX FIFO prior to the transaction.

    3. Consequently, the telemetry received during source.write(D_k) reflects the state under the previous duty cycle \(D_{k-1}\) (or earlier).

    4. SEPIC Dynamics & Sign Inversion:

    5. On a SEPIC converter, reflected input resistance is given by: $\(R_{\text{in}} = R_{\text{load}} \left(\frac{1 - D}{D}\right)^2\)$

    6. Raising duty \(D\) decreases panel voltage \(V_{\text{in}}\) (pulling more current).

    7. Lowering duty \(D\) increases panel voltage \(V_{\text{in}}\) towards \(V_{oc}\).

    8. Inverted Perturbation Feedback:

    9. If the algorithm increases \(D\) to drop the voltage towards \(V_{\text{mpp}}\), but immediately reads stale telemetry where the voltage hasn't dropped yet, it miscalculates power slope \(\frac{\mathrm{d}P}{\mathrm{d}V}\) or conductance increment \(\frac{\mathrm{d}I}{\mathrm{d}V} + \frac{I}{V}\).
    10. The algorithm incorrectly deduces that increasing \(D\) did not yield the expected power change, flipping its perturbation direction to decrease \(D\).
    11. Decreasing \(D\) drives the SEPIC switch towards 0% duty, which raises the panel voltage all the way to \(V_{oc}\).

    2. Expected System Behavior (Single-Measurement on Demand)¶

    To prevent cross-talk between duties and eliminate noise from continuous streaming, the measurement flow follows a strict request-response sequence:

    Detailed Sequence Steps¶

    1. Host/sbc sends a duty to Pico: The Host/sbc calculates the next duty cycle \(D\) and sends it to the Pico via SPI (write(D)).

    2. Pico sets that duty and erases all buffers: The Pico receives the duty, immediately zeroes all measurement registers (MEAS_V_MV, MEAS_I_MA, MEAS_ADC_VOUT_MV, etc.), clears DUTY_SETTLED, and updates the hardware PWM compare register.

    3. Host/sbc has a small delay and erases buffers: The Host/sbc waits a settling window (\(\ge 5-10\,\text{ms}\)) and explicitly flushes its local telemetry cache and SPI input buffers so no cross-talk or stale measurements can leak across duties.

    4. Pico waits the hardware reaction delay: The Pico waits \(2\,\text{ms}\) to allow the SEPIC inductors and coupling capacitors to begin transitioning.

    5. Pico checks if Vin is settled: The Pico monitors \(V_{\text{in}}\) across an 8-sample rolling window until drift is \(< 40\,\text{mV}\) (up to \(80\,\text{ms}\) ceiling).

    6. Pico performs 25-sample filtered measurement: Once steady state is reached, the Pico takes 25 consecutive readings of \(V_{\text{in}}\), \(V_{\text{out}}\), and \(I\):

    7. For each channel, the 25 readings are sorted.

    8. The 4 lowest and 4 highest outliers are discarded (\(25 - 8 = 17\) samples remaining).

    9. The remaining 17 samples are averaged (trimmed mean filter).

    10. Pico holds the single measurement (NO streaming): The Pico stores the filtered mean into MEAS_*, asserts DUTY_SETTLED = true, and parks. The firmware does not run background sampling loops and does not overwrite the filtered result with 1 ms raw readings.

    11. Host/sbc asks for a measurement: Host/sbc sends _CMD_READ_MEASUREMENT (0x02) over SPI.

    12. Pico sends the ONE measurement to the Host/sbc: The Pico returns the single settled measurement frame with is_settled = 1. Upon delivery, Pico clears DUTY_SETTLED = false so it does not stream further data.

    13. Host/sbc analyzes measurements without filtering: The Host/sbc / MPPT algorithm consumes the validated measurement directly, evaluating \(\Delta P\) and \(\Delta V\) with zero host-side filtering or averaging loops.

    4. Filter Specification (25-Sample Trimmed Mean)¶

    Let \(S = [s_0, s_1, \dots, s_{24}]\) be an array of 25 consecutive ADC readings for a given channel (\(V_{\text{in}}\), \(V_{\text{out}}\), or \(I\)):

    1. Sort: $\(S_{\text{sorted}} = \text{sort}(S)\)$
    2. Trim Outliers:
    3. Discard \(S_{\text{sorted}}[0 \dots 3]\) (4 lowest)
    4. Discard \(S_{\text{sorted}}[21 \dots 24]\) (4 highest)
    5. Compute Average: $\(\bar{S} = \frac{1}{17} \sum_{k=4}^{20} S_{\text{sorted}}[k]\)$
